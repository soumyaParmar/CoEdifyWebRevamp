In today’s fast-moving **CPG and FMCG ecosystem**, claims and trade promotions are no longer back-office activities. They directly impact profitability, distributor trust, and execution speed. Yet, many organizations still rely on fragmented workflows, spreadsheets, and manual approvals.

**RevSko changes that.**

## The Problem with Traditional Claims & TPM Systems

For most CPG organizations, claim management looks like this:

- Claims submitted via emails or spreadsheets  
- Manual document verification  
- Long and opaque approval cycles  
- Frequent disputes between distributors and companies  
- Limited reporting and delayed insights  

The result?  
**Revenue leakage, operational inefficiency, and strained distributor relationships.**

---

## How RevSko Solves This

RevSko digitizes and automates the **entire claim and TPM lifecycle**, from creation to settlement—using intelligent workflows and AI-driven validations.

### 1. Structured Claim Creation  
Distributors can raise claims through a guided, form-based interface with predefined claim types, document requirements, and validation rules—eliminating ambiguity from day one.

### 2. Smart Pre-Approvals  
RevSko enables configurable **pre-approval workflows** based on trade type, budget, region, and distributor eligibility. This ensures promotions are approved *before* execution, not after disputes arise.

### 3. AI-Assisted Review & Validation  
Uploaded documents such as invoices, schemes, and ledgers are automatically checked for accuracy, duplication, and policy compliance—dramatically reducing manual effort.

### 4. Controlled Claim Lifecycle  
Once a claim moves forward for review, edits are restricted. This ensures data integrity and eliminates last-minute changes that often lead to conflicts.

### 5. Full Transparency for All Stakeholders  
Every status change, comment, and decision is tracked and visible—giving distributors and internal teams a single source of truth.
